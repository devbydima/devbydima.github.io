Whoa, What Changed?

A memory game for Android and iOS: look at a picture, then spot what changed. More about the game is on the game page, including a free demo room you can play in your browser.

Common questions

  • I bought something and it is missing. Use Restore purchases in the app’s settings. This brings back Remove ads, and your hints and streak freezes, on a new device or after a reinstall. Use the same Apple Account (iOS) or Google account (Android) you bought with. If it still does not show up, e-mail us.
  • How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds go through them: on iOS via reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android via Google Play’s refund help.
  • Where is my progress stored? On your own device. There are no accounts. If you use iCloud Backup (iOS) or Google backup (Android), your device may include it there. Progress does not move between iOS and Android.
  • How do I reset my progress? Choose Reset progress in the settings. Deleting the app removes it too.
  • How do I change my ad or usage statistics choice? Open Settings → Privacy options in the app. On iOS you can also change tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • The daily reminder does not show up. Check that notifications are allowed for the app in your device’s settings, and that the reminder is switched on in the app.
  • Does it need internet? No, the game works offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.
